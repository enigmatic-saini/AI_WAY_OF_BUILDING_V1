# LIGHTS UP — Diwali on the Beat (DJ Mix, English)

*(BPM ~128 · big-room EDM + dhol break + sparkler risers · English with crowd calls · for anyone, anywhere, who's ever lit a lamp against the dark)*

> Track 15 was Hinglish and argued its way through seven lenses. Track 16 is the same night with the lights turned outward — English, so the whole floor can shout it. One lens only this time: **Radha Deep** — *does it create joy for a first-time listener?* If you've never heard of Diwali, you should still know what to do with your hands by the first drop.

---

**[INTRO — single sparkler, hi-hat ticking]**
> One little light... in a whole lot of dark.
> (that's all it takes — that's where we start)
> Count it with me — one... two... a hundred... a thousand—

**[BUILD-UP — riser, dhol creeping in]**
> Every window glowing, every doorway lit,
> the dark had a kingdom — we're taking back a bit.
> Hands to the sky, light in your chest,
> the night that the good got home... LIGHTS UP — here comes the rest!

**[DROP 1 — pataakha hit, 808 + dhol]**
> LIGHTS UP! (hey!) LIGHTS UP! (hey!)
> Every lamp we're lighting says the darkness don't stay!
> LIGHTS UP! (hey!) LIGHTS UP! (hey!)
> Light over dark — that's Diwali — all night, all day!

**[VERSE 1 — bounce, half-sung half-shouted]**
> Clean the house, clean the heart, open up the door,
> sweets on the table, who cares what they're for?
> Doesn't matter where you're from, doesn't matter what you've got —
> one match, one wish — that's the only thing you brought.
> Fourteen years in the forest — the king is coming back,
> every lamp on the road home... that's the first-ever soundtrack!

**[PRE-DROP — crowd, call-and-response]**
> When I say LIGHTS, you say UP!
> LIGHTS! (UP!) LIGHTS! (UP!)
> Sparklers in the air — don't you dare put 'em down—

**[DROP 1 — repeat, everyone]**

**[VERSE 2 — brighter, strings under the kick]**
> Raise a glass for the ones who kept the lamp alive,
> for the gran who lit it yearly so the hope could survive.
> Every fuse that's lit, every spark that flies,
> is a thousand-year-old "no" to the dark in our skies.
> Prosperity's not the gold — it's who's sitting at your side,
> and the road is only clear when the ego steps aside.

**[BRIDGE — half-time, everything drops out, one lamp]**
> ...and when the fireworks are over, and the music's gone to bed,
> there's still one little flame — the one you keep inside your head.
> That's the one that counts. That's the one that stays.
> Light it... (light it)... now let's blow the roof away—

**[DROP 2 — biggest, dhol + brass + fireworks sample]**
> LIGHTS UP! (hey!) LIGHTS UP! (hey!)
> A billion little flames and not one of them afraid!
> LIGHTS UP! (hey!) LIGHTS UP! (hey!)
> Good over dark, light over night — HAPPY DIWALI — we made it, okay?!

**[OUTRO — sparkler fizz, single piano note]**
> One little light... in a whole lot of dark.
> Turns out... that was the whole party. 🪔🎧

## Lens note — Radha Deep, *look*

- **First-time listener test** `[seen]`: the hook is two words with a built-in gesture (LIGHTS → UP). No cultural homework needed to join the chant.
- **Story in one couplet** `[seen]`: the forest / king / road-home image gives a newcomer the *why* without a lecture.
- **Friction found** `[seen]`: draft Verse 2 opened on "wealth and gold" — felt like a shopping ad. Re-aimed to "who's sitting at your side"; Kabir's inner-lamp point from Track 15 carried over into the bridge because it earned its place there too.
- **Exit fired:** *nothing should be done* — it hums. Go play it.
