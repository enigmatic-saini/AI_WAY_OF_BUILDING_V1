# DEEPAK RAAG — Diwali Celebration (Hinglish)

*(BPM ~118 → 136 · starts dholki + harmonium, grows into dhol + brass + 808 · Hindi · English · Punjabi · ek Marathi "Morya" · for the whole mohalla — bachche, daadi, DJ, sab)*

> Track 15 ne lens se *bahas* ki. Track 17 ne lens se *banaaya*. Yahan cycles jury nahi — **composer** the.
> Brahma ne beej diya, Krishna ne teen raaste dikhaaye aur ek chuna, Nav Shakti ne teen raat kaate, Tansen ne aag lagaayi aur phir baarish. §Banaav neeche.

## 🪔 THE SONG

**[INTRO — dholki, harmonium, daadi ki aawaaz]**
> *(daadi:)* "Beta, diya baahar rakh... hawa se bacha ke."
> *(bachcha:)* "Aur agar bujh gaya?"
> *(daadi:)* "Toh doosre se jala lena. Isliye toh sau rakhte hain."
> — *dhol enters* —

**[MUKHDA — mid-tempo, sab saath]**
> Diya se diya, haath se haath,
> ek jale toh sau jalein — yahi Diwali ki baat!
> Andhera kitna bhi ho — darr nahi, yaar,
> roshni ek ki nahi hoti — roshni hai **parivaar**!

**[ANTARA 1 — Kaalratri ki raat (andhera pehle)]**
> Pehle raat kaali thi — Kaalratri ka raaj,
> koi deadline, koi bimaari, koi bichhda hua kaaj.
> Maine kaha "chhod, iss saal mann nahi" —
> daadi boli — "isi saal toh zaroori hai, beta — sun sahi:
> jab mann na ho, tab diya jalaana — wahi asli Diwali hai,
> khushi mein sab jalaate hain... dukh mein jo jalaaye, wo wali hai."

**[MUKHDA]**

**[ANTARA 2 — Mahagauri ki safai (ghar aur dil)]**
> Jhaadu uthi, jaale gire, purana saamaan gaya,
> saath mein wo purana gussa bhi — chupke se nikal gaya.
> Deewarein dhul gayi, khidkiyaan khul gayi — hawa aayi nayi,
> ghar saaf hua toh pata chala... dil mein bhi jagah thi kayi.
> Rangoli ke rang zameen pe — aur chehre pe bhi aaye,
> Mahagauri muskuraayi — "ab Maa ko bulaaye?"

**[BRIDGE — tempo uthta hai, 118 → 136, dhol roll]**
> Ganpati pehle — (Morya!)
> Lakshmi Maa aaye — (Jai ho!)
> Ram ji ghar aaye — (Siya-Ram!)
> ab roko mat — ab bajne do — DEEPAK RAAG!

**[DROP — Deepak raag: aag, brass, 808, pataakhe]**
> DIYA SE DIYA! HAATH SE HAATH!
> ek jale toh sau jalein — poora mohalla saath!
> Chak de phatte — anaar, phuljhadi, chakri ghoom!
> Daadi bhi naachi — "oye hoye!" — DJ ne bola — BOOM!
> Light it up! Light it up! Har gali, har ghar!
> Andhera bhaaga — Diwali aayi — DEEPAK RAAG — har sur!

**[ANTARA 3 — Megh Malhar: baarish, thanda, half-time]**
> *(sab shor ruk jaata hai — sirf harmonium, aur ek diya)*
> Pataakhe thame, dhuaan chhata, raat thodi thehar gayi,
> chhat pe baithe, chai ke saath — baat wo keh gayi:
> "Roshni ka matlab ye nahi ki andhera kabhi na ho,
> roshni ka matlab hai — andhere mein bhi *koi* toh ho."
> Diya se diya... haath se haath...
> *(daadi ki aawaaz, dheere:)* "Dekha? Bujha nahi."

**[FINAL MUKHDA — full, par ab thehraav ke saath]**
> Diya se diya, haath se haath,
> ek jale toh sau jalein — yahi Diwali ki baat!
> Andhera kitna bhi ho — darr nahi, yaar,
> roshni ek ki nahi hoti — roshni hai **parivaar**!

**[OUTRO — dholki wapas, bachche ki aawaaz]**
> *(bachcha:)* "Daadi — agle saal main sau rakhunga."
> *(daadi:)* "Sau nahi beta... ek, par roz."
> 🪔🪕
