## Suno tips
- Pehli generation mein `[Drop]` ko kabhi-kabhi Suno gaata hai — agar aisa ho, us section ko `[Instrumental Break]` + ek chant line bana do.
- Hinglish spelling phonetic rakhi hai (*jala*, *gali*, *balle*) — Suno Devanagari se zyada Roman phonetic pe sahi uchchaaran karta hai.
- Agar female lead chahiye: style prompt mein "male lead" → "female lead, playful".
